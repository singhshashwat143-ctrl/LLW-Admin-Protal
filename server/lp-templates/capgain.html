02Why algorithmic

Three principles, not a strategy

What separates a rule-based approach from discretionary trading — whichever rules it runs.

Rules, not opinions

Every entry and exit is decided before the market opens. No FOMO, no revenge trades, no second-guessing at 3:15.

Risk set in advance

A maximum loss is fixed for each position before it is placed — not decided after the market has moved.

A record you can audit

Every action is logged. You can see what was done, when, and why — and check it against its own rules.

We won’t teach a specific strategy or share trade setups. The aim is that you leave able to evaluate any automated options approach on its own merits — and to know which questions to ask.
